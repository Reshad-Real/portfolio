      U1
    

    

      

        

          I'm an Electrical & Electronic Engineering graduate of BRAC University working at the
          transistor level: TCAD device modelling, sub-5 nm FinFET architectures, and digital-twin-driven
          optimisation of aggressively scaled nodes.
        

        

          Six peer-reviewed Q1 journal papers so far, with impact factors up to 9.8, covering device
          optimisation, digital-twin systems, and intelligent power electronics. Before that I spent two years
          as a student tutor and mentor in the EEE department, which is where I learned that explaining a
          result carefully is most of the work.
        

        

          The bench and the lecture hall pull in the same direction. This site is built the same way —
          poke at it, it's meant to be poked at.
