About Me

Bridging Research & Innovation

I am an Electrical & Electronic Engineering graduate from BRAC University with a passion for pushing the boundaries of technology. My research spans across AI-powered systems, semiconductor nanoelectronics, and smart grid technologies.

With 5 published papers in Q1 journals (Impact Factors up to 9.8), I bring a rigorous, data-driven approach to solving complex engineering problems — from designing self-powered biosensors to developing GaN-based next-generation transistors.

As a Research Assistant and Fellow at BRAC University, I bridge the gap between simulation-driven insights and real-world hardware implementation.
