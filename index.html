// about

From silicon physics to the classroom

I'm an Electrical & Electronic Engineering graduate from BRAC University with a research focus on semiconductor device design and simulation. My work lives at the transistor level — TCAD-based device modeling, sub-5 nm FinFET architectures, and digital-twin-driven optimization of aggressively scaled nodes.

I've authored six peer-reviewed Q1 journal publications (impact factors up to 9.8), spanning device optimization, digital-twin systems, and intelligent power-electronics. As a former Student Tutor and Mentor in the Department of EEE, I bring the same rigor to teaching that I bring to the lab.

My work bridges VLSI and mixed-signal design with a deep commitment to academia — teaching, research, and innovation in equal measure.

Research & Academic Journey

Research Fellow Active
BRAC University · CREST initiative
May 2026 – Present
  • Leading research on “GaN-Based G4FET Technology: Fabrication, Characterization, Modeling, and Circuit Demonstration.”
  • Designing a 5 nm AlGaN/GaN tri-gate FinFET (AS³-FinFET) in Silvaco Atlas TCAD, applying VLSI short-channel-effect theory and polarization-charge device physics.
  • Applying gate, doping, and spacer engineering to manage electrostatic scaling and interface effects at the sub-5 nm node.
  • Benchmarking against global FinFET/nanosheet approaches, with a roadmap toward compact modeling and circuit-level demonstration.
